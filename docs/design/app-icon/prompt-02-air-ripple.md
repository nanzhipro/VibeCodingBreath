Design a macOS app icon for VibeCodingBreath in a style that aligns with Apple Human Interface aesthetics.

Core idea:
Turn the feeling of a slow, mindful breath into a simple icon symbol. The icon should feel quiet, intelligent, and beautifully controlled.

Primary motif:
- a centered ripple made of 2 to 4 soft circular waves
- the inner wave should feel like a breath source or calm pulse
- the outer waves should suggest air moving outward in a controlled rhythm

Art direction:
- premium Apple-platform visual language
- simplified geometry
- crisp silhouette
- balanced spacing
- subtle depth and translucency
- soft atmospheric blue-cyan tones with neutral support colors
- looks excellent in small sizes

Composition:
- one dominant icon object
- no surrounding scene
- no complex lighting gimmicks
- calm square composition suitable for macOS
- visually stable and easy to recognize at a glance

Material feel:
- layered glass or polished translucent enamel
- understated highlights only
- system-friendly appearance

Avoid:
- text
- literal breathing instructions
- busy textures
- harsh neon cyberpunk lighting
- overly glossy old-style skeuomorphism
- abstract art that loses recognizability

The result should feel like a native macOS icon for a meditation-adjacent utility used by developers.
